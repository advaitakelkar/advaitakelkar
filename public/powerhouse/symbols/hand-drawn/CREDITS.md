# Hand-drawn Powerhouse symbol family

Version 2: 44 original SVG symbols redrawn with irregular curved contours, a heavier rounded marker and selected solid silhouettes. Direction follows Adi’s supplied reference: black, handmade, simple and expressive. No source artwork was traced or copied. Built with Codex; no third-party icon set or image-generation credits used. Previous geometric badge family is retained separately.

Use the standalone SVG for print and web; transparent PNG exports are included. The 64 × 64 viewBox is shared by the whole family. Use at 28–40px or larger to keep vehicle and architectural detail legible. These are thesis illustrations, not a replacement for regulated accessibility/transport signage. Website UI icons remain in the established house family.
