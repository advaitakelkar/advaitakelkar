# Powerhouse symbols and programme diagram

44 original SVG symbols constructed for this project with Codex. No external icon library, stock imagery, image-generation credits or third-party icon assets used. The lightning bolt and circular badge follow Adi’s supplied visual reference. No claim of trademark ownership or registered wayfinding compliance is made.

Both sets share a 64 × 64 viewBox: transparent black glyphs and white glyphs in black circular badges. Labels stay outside the icons. Use SVG for InDesign/Illustrator and websites; PNG exports are provided for convenience. Keep clear space of at least one eighth of the badge diameter. Recommended badge size: 32–64 px on web, 8–16 mm in print. Detailed transport glyphs read best at 32 px or larger. Preserve aspect ratio.

Figure 1.6 is conceptual: bands indicate length of stay, not measured distance, spatial capacity or exclusive access. Programme wording retained from the thesis. Research context: thesis section 1.5; Stan Allen, Infrastructural Urbanism (1999). Icons have no independent research authority.

Suggested use: camel → caravanserai; horse and coach → coaching inn; train → railway; aircraft → airport; fuel → filling station; charging → electric forecourt. Use walking and accessibility symbols alongside motorised transport to show public access. Fuel, charging, bus, van, delivery truck, articulated freight, bicycle, e-bike, motorcycle, three-wheeler, car and EV car are separate symbols.
